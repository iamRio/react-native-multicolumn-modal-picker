# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful place for everyone who uses,
discusses, or contributes to it. Participation in the project's GitHub issues,
discussions, pull requests, and other official project spaces is covered by this
Code of Conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and consideration, including when you disagree.
- Keep feedback constructive and focused on ideas, code, and behavior rather
  than personal characteristics.
- Be patient with different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication
  styles.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, listen to feedback, and make a good-faith
  effort to repair harm.
- Respect other people's privacy. Do not share personal or confidential
  information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation,
unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and other conduct
that would make participation unsafe or unwelcoming are not acceptable.

These expectations also apply outside project spaces when someone clearly
speaks or acts on behalf of the project. Examples include using an official
project account, making a statement explicitly for the project, or appearing as
an announced project representative at an event. A person's role alone does
not bring unrelated personal activity within this policy.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainer at
[marcosdelrio.dev@gmail.com](mailto:marcosdelrio.dev@gmail.com). Please include
the relevant links or location, what happened, and any context that will help
with a fair review. Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue or
discussion.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. The maintainer
will respect the reporter's privacy and share report details only as needed to
review and address the situation. Retaliation against someone who raises a
concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this
policy. Depending on the circumstances and severity, responses may include a
private request to stop, a warning, temporary limits on participation, or a
permanent restriction from project spaces. Moderation decisions may include
removing or declining content that violates this policy. Where appropriate,
the reason for a decision will be communicated to the people involved.

If you believe a moderation decision was based on a misunderstanding, you may
request clarification or a review by contacting the maintainer at the reporting
address above and sharing relevant context. The maintainer will consider the
request and explain the decision where appropriate.
